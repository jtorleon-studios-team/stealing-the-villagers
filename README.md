## Intro :

Steal Loot from Villagers in Minecraft

Add a new layer of **villager interaction** with the *Stealing The Villagers* mod! This mod allows players to **loot villagers' pockets** using a unique item called the **Shear of Steal**. Cut the pockets of villagers and collect the loot that falls out, with consequences for your actions.

### Features:

- **Steal Loot from Villagers**: Use the **Shear of Steal** item (right-click) on any villager to steal their loot. Each villager has a unique loot table based on their profession, offering a variety of rewards.
- **Reputation System**: Stealing from villagers comes with a cost! Stealing loot from a villager will reduce your reputation by **-50 points**. By default, you cannot steal from villagers with **less than -25 reputation**, but you can customize this in the configuration.
- **Compatible with Mods**: Works seamlessly with mods like **More Villagers** and **Better Villages**, expanding the possibilities for looting and creating new interactions in your Minecraft world.
- **Customizable Loot Tables**: If you want to modify or add new loot tables, the mod allows full customization.

### Summary:

- **Steal Loot** from villagers with unique loot tables for each profession.
- **Negative Reputation** for stealing actions; manage the reputation points and set your limits.
- **Customizable Loot Tables** via GitHub.
- **Compatible** with popular mods like **More Villagers** and **Better Villages**.
